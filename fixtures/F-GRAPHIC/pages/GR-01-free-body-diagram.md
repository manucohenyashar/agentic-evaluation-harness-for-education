A crate is drawn as a square resting on a line inclined 25 degrees above the horizontal.
Three arrows leave the centre of the square:
  - an arrow labelled W pointing vertically downward
  - an arrow labelled N pointing away from the ramp surface, perpendicular to it

The incline angle is marked at the base with the label 25 deg.
